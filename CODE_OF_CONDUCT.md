# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, volunteer-run effort. We want it to be a place where
people can ask questions, report problems, and contribute code without
feeling unwelcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith. People often make mistakes or lack context.
- Keep discussion on the technical problem, not on the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with maintainers who have limited time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including health data,
  tokens, or endpoint URLs, without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after it has been addressed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests, and may block
people who break these expectations. They will try to be fair and to explain
their decision where that is practical.

## Reporting a problem

Report conduct problems privately, using the same channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): the GitHub advisory form at
https://github.com/ctribuna/garmin-mcp/security/advisories/new. Please do not
post conduct concerns in public issues or pull requests.
